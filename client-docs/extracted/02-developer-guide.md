**STEP 6 — THINGS YOU MUST NEVER DO**
Read this section carefully. These mistakes are common and can seriously damage the site.
## Never Do These Things
**DO NOT create state-by-state duplicate pages**
**Example of what NOT to do:**
•  Do not create /excavator-transport-florida/
•  Do not create /excavator-transport-georgia/
•  Do not create /excavator-transport-alabama/
...with the same words just replacing the state name. Google calls this doorway page abuse and can penalize the entire site. Build strong service pages first. Use Google Ads data later to decide if any specific state pages are truly needed.
**DO NOT send all Google Ads traffic to the homepage**
Every ad must go to the specific service page that matches what the person searched. If someone searched "boat transport" and clicks the ad, they must land on the boat transport page — not the homepage. Homepage ad traffic converts at 0.5%. Service page traffic converts at 4-9%. This is a huge difference in money.
**DO NOT use Broad Match keywords in Google Ads**
Broad match will show ads for completely wrong searches and drain the entire budget in days. Always use [Exact Match] and "Phrase Match" only.
**DO NOT run ads without conversion tracking set up**
Without conversion tracking, there is no way to know which keywords are making money. Set up GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking before spending a dollar.
**DO NOT buy cheap backlinks from any SEO company**
If any company offers hundreds of backlinks for a low price, decline. This is called black hat SEO and Google can penalize the site for years. It is not worth it.
**DO NOT copy content from competitor websites**
Google detects duplicate content. Pages with copied text will not rank. All page content must be original.
