### Mobile responsive rules
- One-column stacked layout.
- Keep content width comfortable with roughly 20–24px side padding.
- Minimum body/link text 15–16px.
- Minimum tap target 44px.
- Full-width or nearly full-width CTA button.
- Keep the trust row simple; allow 2x2 wrapping on narrow phones.
- Badge row may wrap 2 per row.
- Center social icons and legal text.
- Avoid overcrowding and excessive glow effects.
