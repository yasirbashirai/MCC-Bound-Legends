# Open Questions for the Client (before build starts)

Send these in one message / kickoff call. Ordered by how much they block the build.

## Blocking
1. **Package decision** — Foundation $740 or Growth $1,130, and the 50% deposit.
2. **The mockup** — your homepage doc says "following the mockup I showed you". Please send it.
3. **Orange brand color** — exact hex code, or any brand/logo file you have.
4. **Logo** — do you have a logo file, or is it the text "MCC Bound Legends" with Bound in orange?
5. **Where do leads go?** — email address(es) and the mobile number for SMS alerts.

## Claims we need confirmed (they appear on every page)
6. **BBB** — are you BBB accredited? Send your BBB profile link. If not, we remove it from the hero, trust strips and badges.
7. **Review count** — copy says "hundreds of 5-star reviews"; your site shows 9. What number can we truthfully say?
8. **"Guaranteed"** in the headline — guaranteed what? (price locked, pickup date, fully insured?) We'll footnote it.
9. **Comparison table** — can you confirm each ✗ for Montway and AmeriFreight is accurate today (Montway lists boat and RV transport on their site)? Alternative: compare against "typical car-only shippers" without naming them.
10. **Military discount** — what is the actual discount?
11. **Hawaii and Alaska** — do you actually book those, or "48 states"?

## Content
12. **Photos** — your trucks, trailers, boats and equipment on the trailer. Real photos beat stock for Ads Quality Score.
13. **Service page body text** — the guide gives a template, not the words. Do you want us to write all 10 service pages, About, FAQ, How It Works? (We recommend yes.)
14. **Expedited transport** — it's on the homepage grid but missing from the page list. Build it in Phase 1? (We recommend yes, plus Military.)
15. **Local pages** — your guide says no state pages. Our Growth package had 8 city/route pages; we propose swapping them for your 11 Phase 2 vehicle pages. OK?

## Technical / access
16. **Hosting** — stay on Hostinger WordPress, or let us host the new site (faster, no plugins to break)? Do you need to edit pages yourself?
17. **Access** — Google Ads, Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console, domain registrar (for DNS), current hosting.
18. **Who runs the Google Ads?** — the guide has 5 campaigns and ~$130–190/day at full rollout. We can quote campaign setup + management separately if you want.
19. **Homepage form position** — your homepage doc puts the form below the vehicle cards; your guide says the form must be visible without scrolling on every page. We suggest the form sits in the hero on desktop. OK?
